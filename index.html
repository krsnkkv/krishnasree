About

I grew up in Dubai in a family of Carnatic musicians, studied physics, chemistry, maths and biology at school, and came to computer science sideways. In July 2026 I finished an MEng in Computer Science and Software Engineering at the University of Birmingham with First Class Honours: three years at the Dubai campus, then a final year at Edgbaston.

I'm now at the University of Edinburgh's School of Informatics for an MSc in Artificial Intelligence. I care about interpretability: understanding why a model gives the answer it does, and whether its reasoning looks anything like ours. That pulls me towards cognitive science and neuroscience as much as machine learning. Longer term I want to do doctoral research on cognitively informed AI and Theory of Mind.

Outside research I co-founded an AR travel app, led creative and operations at an indie game studio, and ran a public workshop on whether AI can be creative.

Research

I want models we can open up and question, and I use human cognition as the yardstick: if a model's reasoning can be laid out in terms people use, we can check it against how people actually think. My dissertation tested that idea on music, a domain I know from the inside.
